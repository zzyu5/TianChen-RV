### SHARPENED (2026-06-30, q4_0 implement attempt hit the wall + STOPped): q4_0 e2e = route-ABI-ARITY widening, NOT a fact-stamp fold-in
An implement attempt investigated past the route-recording gap and hit a structural wall the dequant arc never faced:
- **The q4_0 offset-binary product (`PackedI4OffsetBinaryXI8ProductOp`) is a 3-INPUT op** (weight + two activation halves `qlo`/`qhi`; the front door `RVVPackedI4DotSourceFrontDoor.cpp:575,578` assigns BOTH `qlo` and `qhi` the `rhs-input-buffer` role).
- **The production product-reduction route is a 2-INPUT (lhs × rhs) ABI model.** `assignRVVGenericLoadBinding` (`RVVEmitCRouteConfigBinding.cpp:2763-2765`) rejects the 2nd `rhs-input-buffer` ("requires a unique rhs-input-buffer load"). The `productSlot*` accessor family (`RVVEmitCRoutePlanning.h:225-288`) is hardwired to `getLhs()`/`getRhs()` — no 3rd slot. The product-reduction structural validation (`RVVEmitCRouteAnalysis.cpp:3858-3924`) asserts `productSlotLhs==lhsValue && productSlotRhs==rhsValue`, 2-operand throughout.
- **Why dequant was clean:** it reused the existing 2-operand `WideningProductOp` head (same lhs/rhs/2-load ABI) and only flipped LMUL WIDTH (m2/m4↔m1/m2) within that arity. The nibble-unpack product is also 2-operand. q4_0 offset-binary is the FIRST 3-input product head.
- **Closing q4_0 requires a 7-step route-IDENTITY arity change** (not just "more files"): new slice field + 3rd input-buffer slot for `activation_high`; extend the `productSlot*` accessors from 2- to 3-operand; new runtime-ABI role / disambiguation for two rhs loads; extend the product-reduction structural validation to 3 operands; a new 3-input `widening_product_multiplicand_roles` ABI-order fact (current encodes a 2-multiplicand `lhs=…;rhs=…` identity); standalone-reduce promotion gate + c-type/relation vocab; THEN the front-door fact-stamp.
- **Codebook is strictly larger** (two brand-new op families `CodebookTableBroadcast`+`CodebookGatherXI8Product`, zero export references) — and likely also non-2-operand.
**Net: q4_0/codebook production-export e2e is a deliberate route-ABI-arity-widening design arc (a 3-input product-reduction route identity), NOT a dequant-style fact-stamp follow-up. It deserves its own design pass, fresh context. The dequant body remains the single proven end-to-end production-reachable Track-B body.**

### The "restructure q4_0 to 2-operand" escape is RULED OUT (2026-06-30 recon — the 3 inputs are ESSENTIAL)
Investigated whether the q4_0 body could be restructured to a 2-operand form (reusing the existing route) instead of widening the route ABI. **Verdict: NOT viable — the 3-input shape is genuinely essential, not authorial.**
- The op (`PackedI4OffsetBinaryXI8ProductOp`, `RVVOps.td:3814-3871`) is ASYMMETRIC + one-sided: weight (packed-i4, nibble-decoded internally via xor 0x88) + **TWO PLAIN-i8 activation operands** (`activation_low`/`activation_high`, NOT decoded). Weight-low-nibble × activation_low, weight-high-nibble × activation_high (`vwmul` + `vwmacc`). Verifier hard-requires exactly 4 operands (weight + 2 activations + vl).
- The 2 activation buffers are intrinsic to the **ggml q4_0_q8_0 format** (`q8_low`/`q8_high` are SEPARATE memrefs — a memory-layout property, not bit-packing). The front door must accept the standard ggml q4_0_q8_0 6-param source ABI (`RVVPackedI4DotSourceFrontDoor.cpp:159-167`).
- Cannot reuse the 2-operand `PackedI4NibbleUnpackProductOp` (it requires BOTH operands packed-i4 and unpacks both; here only the weight is packed, activations are plain). Cannot internally unpack a single plain activation (nothing to unpack). Cannot merge the two activation buffers without changing the ggml ABI.
**So q4_0 e2e has NO clean shortcut — it genuinely requires widening the shared 2-operand route ABI to a 3-operand (N-operand) product-reduction route identity.** ⚠ That shared route ABI is exactly what the dequant e2e (`ed4a8424`/`43932670`/`59b08f9a`) depends on — so the arity-widening must be done carefully to not regress the proven dequant path. This is a deliberate architectural arc (and arguably a design-philosophy call: generalize the route to N-operand products), best done fresh-context, NOT on a deep tail. The dequant body stays the single proven production-reachable Track-B body.
