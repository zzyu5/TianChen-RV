## Ambiguity flags (task-required "flag anything ambiguous")
1. **`status` / `availability` in-shape? — GENUINE FORK, escalate.** Both are typed **closed-enum fields** on every `CapabilityDescriptor` (`status` = I32Enum `ExecOps.td:42–57`; `availability` = C++ enum `CapabilityModel.h:21`), yet the `[S-5]` item ① field enumeration (`capability-contract.md:90` = `id/kind/subclass/implies/conflicts/params/provenance/trust`) **omits them**. Two readings:
   - **In-shape (likely):** they are typed fact-fields like the rest; the `[S-5]` shorthand simply didn't list them, and `[S-2]` semantics ("missing status 视为 available", "status 优先于 availability") depend on the closed enum. → declare their field + closed enum in schema.def item ①.
   - **Out-of-shape:** `status`/`availability` describe whether a *particular* fact is present/available on a *particular* target — arguably per-instance **content** (like param *values*), not the declared shape.
   Recommend **in-shape** (they are typed structural fields), but this is a decision for the human, not to resolve by fiat.
2. **Item ③ vs `[S-2]`**: the *relation-type table* (shape) is in schema.def; the *transitive-closure computation* is loading-time code, NOT shape. Keep separate so an `implies`-closure fix does not read as a shape change.
3. **`profile` providers**: a `tcrv.exec.target` profile is a *provider* (carries `provides`, **no** capability-fact `kind`) that expands to a normalized fact set at load; `declared-instance-hash` is taken over the *expanded* set (`capability-contract.md:65`). Profiles are content, not shape — confirm they stay out of schema.def.
4. **`subclass`/`provenance`/`trust`**: declared in the *shape* (in-shape) even though no code producer exists yet — see scope summary "declared-with-no-producer".
